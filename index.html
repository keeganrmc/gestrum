01 // THE CORE EXPERIENCE

Drumming in mid-air should make real sound.

We have all found ourselves tapping on a steering wheel, drumming our fingers across a desk, or playing invisible air-drums to our favorite record. The physical reflex is universal—yet until now, moving your hands in the air produced nothing but silence.

⚡ Turn Your Camera into a Stage Instrument

Gestrum turns the standard webcam already resting in your laptop or monitor into an expressive, low-latency percussion instrument. There are no gloves to wear, no plastic controllers to charge, no MIDI cables to route, and no DAW software to configure.

When you launch Gestrum, six luminous, translucent sound pads hover across a distraction-free dark stage in an intuitive 3x2 grid. Simply raise your hands. The vision engine identifies your index fingertips, rendering them as floating neon orbs with fluid motion trails. Striking downward into any pad triggers an immediate percussive punch and an expansive radial shockwave.

🎛️ Velocity Sensitivity & Dwell Looping

Gestrum doesn't just treat your hands as on/off switches. Real-time velocity algorithms track the downward speed of your strike: a light tap triggers a gentle ghost note with a subtle ripple, while a fast, deliberate strike unleashes high-impact attack power and maximum visual bloom.

When you find a groove you want to build on, hold your fingertip stationary inside a pad for three seconds. A gold circular progress gauge fills around the pad rim and locks it into an automated rhythmic loop synchronized to the master 16-step measure bar. Tap the spacebar or click "Pause All" at any time for instant silence.

02 // DEEP DIVE

Built for tactile performance, live looping, and sound design.

Every subsystem in Gestrum is purpose-built to deliver immediate acoustic response, visual clarity, and creative freedom.

👐

Touchless Spatial Tracking

Powered by Google MediaPipe Hands running locally via WebAssembly and WebGL. Tracks 21 3D skeletal landmarks per hand with fingertip collision detection. Your hands are rendered as floating luminous orbs with subtle particle trails—keeping the stage focused, theatrical, and free of raw webcam clutter.

MediaPipe Vision Multi-Hand
⏱️

3-Second Dwell-to-Loop

Hold any fingertip inside a pad for three seconds to lock it into automated continuous playback. Each pad can loop at independent rhythmic subdivisions (1/4, 1/8, 1/16, or 1/32 notes) locked to a master 16-step visual measure bar. Hit the Spacebar or "Pause All" at any moment to silence the room.

16-Step Quantized Dwell Latch
🎨

Three Complete Themes & Kits

Switch between three distinct acoustic and visual worlds: Hip-Hop / 808 (Cyber Neon, 120 BPM) with sub kicks and trap snares; Rock & Roll (Crimson Stage, 132 BPM) with heavy acoustic beater kicks and overdriven power chords; and Midnight Jazz (Velvet Lounge, 100 BPM) with brushed snares, cross-sticks, upright bass, and vibraphone with rotating tremolo.

🔊

25+ Procedural Presets & Custom Samples

Every pad is powered by real-time Web Audio API procedural synthesis—combining custom oscillators, noise generators, biquad filter sweeps, and waveshaper distortion with zero audio file dependencies. Want your own sounds? Simply drag and drop any WAV or MP3 audio file directly onto a pad to make it your own.

Movable Pads & Acoustic Size Scaling

Switch to Layout Mode to drag pads anywhere on the screen, matching your chair height, camera lens angle, or arm span. Pick from 5 rounded shapes (Circle, Square, Triangle, Diamond, Heart). Resizing a pad dynamically scales its acoustic shell: larger pads produce deeper sub tones and longer decays, while smaller pads yield crisp, high-frequency snap.

03 // QUICK START GUIDE

How to start playing in 30 seconds.

No manuals, no accounts, and no hardware calibration required. Follow these four steps to start making beats immediately.

01

Position Your Space & Check Lighting

Sit or stand comfortably roughly 2 to 3 feet in front of your laptop or desktop webcam. Make sure your room has decent ambient light so your hands stand out against your background. Avoid sitting with an overly bright window directly behind you.

02

Launch Gestrum & Enable the Camera

Click Launch Gestrum below. In the top navigation bar, click the Camera button to grant local webcam permission. Look at the bottom-left status indicator: when it glows green, on-device vision is active. (Camera optional: you can also tap pads with a mouse or touchscreen anytime).

03

Strike the Air to Trigger Drum Hits

Bring your hands into view. Your index fingertips appear as glowing luminous orbs. Strike downward into any of the six floating pads. Hit firmly for punchy, dynamic strikes, or tap gently for softer ghost hits.

04

Hold for 3 Seconds to Lock in a Loop

Hold your fingertip steady inside any pad. Watch the golden progress ring complete around the pad perimeter. Once full, the pad locks into continuous playback on the beat. Try layering multiple pads, switch themes via the header dropdown, or tap the Spacebar to pause all active loops.

04 // STORY & PHILOSOPHY

Who made this, how it works, and why it exists.

A look into the motivation, craft, and architectural decisions behind an embodied web instrument.

The Origin: Turning Mid-Air Motion into Sound

Gestrum was created by Keegan McConnell, a design student and creative technologist exploring embodied physical interaction on the web. It grew directly out of the project proposal AirDrummer.

Almost every music fan has found themselves air-drumming along to a song. The impulse is instinctive, joyous, and kinetic. Yet for decades, digital musical instruments have demanded mechanical compromise: tapping rigid plastic keys, clicking computer mice, or strapping on bulky specialized sensors.

Gestrum exists to answer a simple creative question: Why shouldn't air-drumming make real sound? By utilizing the standard webcam that already exists on millions of everyday laptops, Gestrum transforms open physical space into a zero-friction musical stage.

How It Was Engineered: Single-File PWA Architecture

Rather than relying on heavy server infrastructure, proprietary frameworks, or complex build toolchains, Gestrum was built adhering to the strict creative coding standard of the Single-File Progressive Web App:

  • Zero Backend Infrastructure: There are no external databases, user accounts, or cloud API endpoints. Everything required to run the instrument—the visual canvas, audio DSP synthesizers, gesture trackers, and project management—is packaged in self-contained client-side code.
  • Native Web Audio DSP: Sounds are synthesized mathematically in real time using native Web Audio API oscillators, white noise buffers, biquad filter topologies, and custom soft-clipping waveshaping transfer curves. This delivers instant, zero-latency acoustic response without waiting for large audio sample packs to download over the network.
  • Local Machine Learning Vision: Hand tracking is executed entirely on the user's local CPU/GPU using Google's MediaPipe Hands WebAssembly/WebGL models.
  • Local-First Persistence: Project files, custom audio sample uploads, and layout preferences are saved directly to browser IndexedDB, persisting indefinitely across browser refreshes and offline sessions.

Privacy by Design: Your Camera Never Leaves Your Machine

We take user privacy seriously. In Gestrum, your webcam video stream is analyzed frame-by-frame in volatile local browser memory and immediately discarded. No video frames, images, or audio recordings are ever transmitted over the internet, stored in the cloud, or accessible to anyone else. Gestrum contains zero tracking cookies, zero marketing analytics scripts, and zero third-party telemetry.

06 // WHAT'S NEXT

Future roadmap & planned capabilities.

Gestrum is an evolving creative tool. Here is a grounded look at upcoming features currently planned for future releases.

PLANNED // V0.7

16-Step Pattern Sequencer Grid per Pad

Upgrading the dwell-loop engine to allow per-pad 16-step rhythmic grids. Instead of fixed-interval pulses, players will be able to draw custom syncopated patterns, polyrhythms, and ghost notes directly on each pad.

PLANNED // V0.8

Web MIDI Input & Output Integration

Connecting external physical MIDI drum pads and controllers to trigger Gestrum's synth engines, and routing Gestrum's touchless mid-air gesture hits outward as MIDI messages into DAWs like Ableton Live, Logic Pro, and Reaper.

PLANNED // V0.9

Articulated Air Gestures & Cymbal Chokes

Expanding gesture vocabulary beyond simple strikes: a two-finger pinch gesture in mid-air to instantly choke a ringing crash cymbal, vertical hand distance for continuous filter sweeps, and rapid two-handed drum rolls.

PLANNED // V1.0

Curated Sound Pack Library

Expanding procedural preset banks to include dedicated genre collections: 90s Boom-Bap, Lo-Fi Chillhop, Afrobeat percussion, and Industrial Synthwave, with custom acoustic decay parameters per kit.

PLANNED // V1.1

Lighting & Camera Calibration Wizard

A gentle visual calibration overlay that tests ambient room lighting, recommends optimal distance, and fine-tunes detection thresholds for challenging angles and backlit conditions.
